{{condition}} Product condition
{{category}} Category
{{tags}} Tags (for Vinted: as #hashtags)
{{description}} Your description text
{{extraInfo}} Additional info (brand, size, etc.)
{{brand}} Brand (from AI analysis)
{{size}} Size (from AI analysis)
